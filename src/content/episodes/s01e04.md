Every piece of a pipeline promises to deliver at least once: the scheduler, the queue, the retry. At least once means sometimes twice, and the place the two runs finally collide is the write to S3. S3 has no lock. Two writers to one key, and the last one wins, silently. It does have a 412, and since 2024 the two request headers that turn a plain write into “only if nobody has”, which is enough to build the lock yourself.
