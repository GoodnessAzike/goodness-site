In part 1, a file that moves from one place to another in production is moved by a worker, and AWS calls that worker compute. To do its job the worker needs permission from S3, and it gets that by wearing a role. On every request S3 reads the role and the bucket’s own rules, and a deny on either side wins. The data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
