S3 is a dictionary the size of a planet: buckets hold objects by key. In production, nobody uploads a file by hand. Something runs your code and does the writing, on a schedule, every night. AWS calls that something compute, four kinds matter to a data engineer, and they make three moves. Part 1 is the workers. Part 2 is permissions. Part 3 is networking, and the bill.
